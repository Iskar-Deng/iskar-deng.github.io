Hi! I’m Iskar Deng.

I am a graduate of the master’s program in Computational Linguistics at the University of Washington, where I was advised by Prof. Shane Steinert-Threlkeld. I am currently collaborating with Shane in the CLMBR lab. Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree in Computational and Applied Linguistics from Peking University.

My work combines computational linguistics and cognitive science. I study how language models align with and diverge from humans in learning and using linguistic structure, especially in typological preferences. I am also interested in mechanistic interpretability and explainable AI.

Outside research, I play baseball and support the Seattle Mariners.
